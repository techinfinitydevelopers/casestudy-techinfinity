The occasion

A new brand gets one first introduction.

CEER is Saudi Arabia's first EV brand. On 21 September 2026 it revealed EXOBOT, its first flagship sedan and SUV. It was the biggest moment in the brand's history. We helped it look the part.

No car.

CEER designed and engineered EXOBOT. We built it in 3D from scratch, then lit and rendered it for launch.
